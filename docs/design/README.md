# Design notes

Version: **1.0.0** · Date: 2026-09-26

Design notes and working documents for octools: sketches, plans, research,
and reviews. They are unpublished and are not decision records; decisions
live in [`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/README.md) as MADR records.

A note meant to outlive the work it supports says so under its H1, and only
such a note may be cited by a decision record or a published page. Working
notes point at the decision record they feed, never the other way round.
